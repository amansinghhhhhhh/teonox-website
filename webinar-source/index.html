Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the workshop.

I'm confused about what career path to choose. Can this workshop help me?
Absolutely. You'll explore different paths across AI, Digital Marketing, Content, Creator Economy, Freelancing, Growth and Entrepreneurship - and understand what each path looks like, what skills you need and what you can do next.
Do I need any prior knowledge of AI or Digital Marketing?
Not at all. The workshop is designed to be beginner-friendly. You'll be introduced to the concepts, tools and real-world applications from the ground up.
Is this workshop only for students who want to become Digital Marketers?
No. AI and Digital Marketing can open multiple career directions. Whether you're interested in building a career, becoming a creator, freelancing, starting a business or exploring AI, you'll find relevant insights here.
What will I actually learn at the workshop?
You'll explore Business, Digital Marketing and AI through practical concepts and real-world use cases, including content, audience research, growth, online business and AI-powered workflows.
Will I get to actually do something during the workshop?
Yes. This isn't designed to be a sit-and-listen session. You'll take part in interactive activities, practical exercises and an interesting challenge where you can apply your thinking.
What kind of challenge will there be?
There'll be an interesting real-world challenge or activity designed to get you thinking, creating and applying what you learn. The challenge can vary, so expect something you won't see coming.
Will I actually create something I can use later?
You'll get an opportunity to work on something practical that can potentially be developed further into a project or portfolio piece.
What career options can I explore after learning these skills?
You could explore paths such as AI Marketing, Digital Marketing, Content Creation, Growth, Freelancing, Entrepreneurship and other emerging digital roles.
What will I take home from the workshop?
More than notes. You'll leave with practical insights, resources, new ideas, exposure to AI tools, a better understanding of possible career paths and clarity on what you can learn or build next.
Will I receive a certificate?
Yes. Every participant receives a Teonox Workshop Certificate.
Is this workshop useful if I want to start my own business?
Yes. You'll explore how AI, Digital Marketing, content, audience building, lead generation and online growth can help you build and grow a business.
What happens after the workshop?
The goal is to help you understand what to do next - whether that means choosing a career direction, developing a skill, building a project, creating a portfolio or continuing your learning journey.
