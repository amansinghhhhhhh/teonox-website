The idea

Walk In Confused. Walk Out With a Plan.

Don't choose a career because it's trending. Understand it first.

You've probably heard about Digital Marketing. You've seen Instagram ads, influencers, brands going viral and businesses growing online. But have you ever wondered what actually happens behind all of it?

Who creates these strategies? Who runs campaigns? Who understands customer behaviour? And what does it really take to build a career in this industry?

Marketing Uncovered is a 90-minute interactive workshop designed to help you explore the world of marketing, understand its career opportunities and discover whether this industry is the right fit for you.

The agenda

What Will You Uncover?

Five focused parts. One clear picture of what a marketing career actually looks like.

01

The World of Marketing

Go beyond social media posts and advertisements. Understand what marketing really is, how businesses use it and why it plays a crucial role in today's economy.

02

Career Opportunities You Didn't Know Existed

Explore different career paths, including:

  • Performance Marketing
  • Search Engine Optimisation (SEO)
  • Social Media Marketing
  • Content & Brand Strategy
  • Marketing Analytics
  • AI-Powered Marketing
03

Is Marketing Right for You?

Are you creative? Analytical? Curious about consumer behaviour? Interested in business and technology? Understand the mindset and skills required to succeed in marketing and discover whether your interests align with this industry.

04

What Companies Actually Look For

Discover what businesses expect from marketing professionals, why practical skills matter and what makes someone employable in this competitive industry.

05

Your Roadmap Into Marketing

Understand how beginners can get started, which skills to develop, how to gain practical experience and how to prepare for real-world opportunities.

Self-check

Is Marketing Uncovered For You?

This workshop is for you if…

  • You've finished Class 12 and are choosing a degree or course
  • You're in BBA, BCom, BMS, BA, BSc or BCA and want skills that earn alongside your degree
  • You're a fresh graduate weighing a job, an MBA, freelancing or a switch into marketing
  • You want to work with clients in India and abroad

You may want something else if…

  • You want to become an AI/ML engineer or software developer — this is applied AI for business
  • You expect guaranteed income or a job from a 90-minute session
  • You're looking for a get-rich-quick shortcut — earning here takes skill and steady work
  • You want to sit back and watch a lecture
Why attend Marketing Uncovered

Because choosing a career is a big decision.

You shouldn't have to invest months of learning or thousands of rupees just to find out whether an industry interests you. This workshop gives you an opportunity to:

  • Understand the reality of the marketing industry.
  • Explore different career possibilities.
  • Identify the skills required.
  • Understand industry expectations.
  • Make a more informed career decision.

No prior marketing knowledge required. Just curiosity and an open mind.
